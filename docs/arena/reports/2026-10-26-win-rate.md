# A higher win rate (WL-137, WL-138, WL-140, WL-142, WL-143)

Sheryar wants a 70 to 80% win rate. Two ways tested with `scripts/research/winrate_paths.py`, on the stored candles of run 14's 150 coins (choose) and run 15's 216 coins (confirm), swing, position and hold together, over all years and from 2022 (the WL-133 rule). Costs 0.04% fee and 0.02% slippage a side.

## WL-138: a win-rate agent, a dip buyer

Long only: RSI 2 under 10 above the 200 average (Connors), times a trend filter (none, 50-bar momentum up, higher timeframe up), an exit (the first close in profit, a close above the 5-bar average, a target 1 ATR up) and a stop of 3 or 6 ATR. 18 variants. The best of each kind, all styles:

| Variant | Choose: win, avg R | Choose from 2022: win, avg R | Confirm: win, avg R | Confirm from 2022: win, avg R |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| no filter, first close in profit (Connors RSI 2 today, long) | 77.5%, -0.024 | 76.8%, -0.037 | 78.3%, -0.003 | 77.6%, -0.013 |
| momentum up, first close in profit | 79.0%, -0.011 | 78.2%, -0.026 | 79.6%, +0.009 | 78.9%, -0.000 |
| momentum up, target 1 ATR, stop 6 ATR | 78.6%, -0.005 | 77.3%, -0.016 | 78.5%, see below | 77.2%, -0.014 |
| momentum up, close above the 5-bar average | 60.8%, -0.018 | 59.9%, -0.031 | 61.6%, +0.005 | 60.7%, -0.004 |

The win rate is there (77 to 84% with the quick exits) but no variant is positive from 2022 on either set: every one loses a few hundredths of an R a trade after costs. The momentum filter helps a little; the higher-timeframe filter does not. Before 2022 they were slightly positive (+0.03 to +0.07R). None passes; no agent was added.

Data note: the 1 ATR target variants show an absurd average from 2022 on run 15's coins (about -3 billion R): a market whose ATR is about zero gives a stop of about zero, so the costs divided by it explode. It does not change the outcome (those variants fail on run 14's coins already); `agents.min_stop_frac` or a cost floor would keep such a market out of a real agent.

## What it shows

A 70 to 80% win rate is easy to build and costs money; a positive average R comes with a 30 to 50% win rate. On these markets and costs, from 2022, no rule tested has both.

## WL-143: the Sniper

Sheryar asked for an agent with a 100% win rate and the best R. `scripts/research/sniper.py` (4 slices, merged): enter only where several profitable agents agree, 2 or 3 of Sheryar's, Momentum's, Turtle's and Gujjar AB=CD's own entry rules firing long on the same coin within 5 or 10 bars (one of them on the entry bar), Bitcoin above its 200-day average, optionally the higher timeframe up; R is 1.5 ATR, the stop 3 or 5 times R, the whole trade out at +0.25R or +0.5R, three times the style's horizon. Long only, major coins and commodities, swing, position and hold. 24 variants.

| Variant | Choose: win, all years, from 2022 | Confirm: win, all years, from 2022 |
|---|---|---|
| 2 of 4 within 5 bars, stop 5x, 0.25R (highest win rate) | 94.6%, -0.019, -0.048 | 94.9%, -0.011, -0.047 |
| 2 of 4 within 10 bars, stop 5x, 0.5R (best all-years R) | 89.7%, +0.005, -0.044 | 90.2%, +0.027, -0.037 |
| 3 of 4 within 10 bars, stop 3x, 0.25R | 92.6%, -0.006, -0.040 | 92.4%, -0.026, -0.042 |
| 3 of 4 within 10 bars, higher timeframe up, stop 5x, 0.5R | 88.2%, -0.073, -0.170 | 88.4%, -0.092, -0.186 |

None passes: every variant wins 84 to 95% and loses from 2022 on both sets. Agreement does not help; three agents agreeing, or the higher timeframe up as well, is worse: when several trend rules fire together the move is usually late. With a 5R stop and a 0.25R target one stop cancels twenty wins, and from 2022 the stops come slightly too often. No agent added; the highest win rate that still makes money is Gujjar AB=CD (High Win Rate), about 90%.
